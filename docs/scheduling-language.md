# Scheduling Language & Pattern Expression Spec

## Relative Time Expressions
- `in 30 minutes`
- `in 2 hours`
- `in 1 day`

## Exact Time Expressions
- `tomorrow at 9 AM`
- `tomorrow at 6 PM`

## Recurring Patterns
- `every day at 8 AM`
- `every weekday at 9 AM`
- `every Monday at 10 AM`
- `every 2 hours`
